## One page for everything you sell

Your page lists your services next to your digital products: a pedicure next to your aftercare guide, a pottery class next to a recorded glazing course. Clients can discover your services through <PlatformName /> Network and buy digital products on your page.

## Requests, not a calendar

Clients request a service and say when they would like it. You confirm the time and the price in the quote, and the client pays a deposit with PayPal. There is no booking calendar yet.

## A checkout that fits what you sell

A service and a digital product follow separate payment flows: the service on its pay page, the product at checkout on your page. Keep both connected to the same business without treating a client brief like a downloadable file.
