## Your own page, paid through PayPal

Give customers one link to your courses and downloads. They choose an item on your page, approve the payment through PayPal and receive a time-limited download link.

## From purchase to delivery

The order records what was bought and whether the payment completed. Your file stays connected to the order, so the customer can get the download they paid for.

## Services alongside your products

If you also sell professional work, show services alongside your products. A service begins with a request and quote; a digital product has its own checkout. Each follows the flow suited to what you sell.

## Bring your catalog

Add your courses and downloads to a <PlatformName /> store. There is no import tool yet, so add the products you want to offer directly.

## What it costs

Store plans start at $12 a month with a 2% fee on each order. Grow is $39 with 1%, and Scale is $129 with 0.5%. PayPal's own fees are separate.
