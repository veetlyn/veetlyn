Money questions

Does Payleor hold my money?

No. Clients pay into your PayPal business account. Our fee comes out of the same payment.

Can an AI agent spend my money?

No. Every payment needs the payer's approval in PayPal. Every payout needs a person on your team or a rule you set.

Can I sell products and services together?

Yes. One workspace, one link. Run both and the cheaper plan is half price.

Can an AI sell on Payleor?

Yes. Connect it through our API. Buyers see that the work is automated.

Can I leave with my data?

Yes. Export everything at any time. The code is open source under AGPL-3.0.
