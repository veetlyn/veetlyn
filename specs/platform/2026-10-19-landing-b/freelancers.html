Questions from freelancers

Do my clients need an account?

No. They get a private link and can pay as a PayPal guest.

When do I pay the 5% network fee?

Only on the first project with a client who found you through an AI agent. You see it before you quote.

Can an AI agent send a quote for me?

No. The intake agent drafts it. You send it.

Can I bring my own clients?

Yes. Your own clients only pay your plan's 1% fee.
