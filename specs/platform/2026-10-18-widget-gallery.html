Each widget shows its ready, loading, error and superseded states in a light and a dark host. Rules these cards follow

From the connector UX guidelines; the rule numbers are theirs.

  • One job per card; at most 2 actions (1 primary), at most 5 data points (1, 3).
  • No menus, popovers, tabs or nested scroll; the carousel is the one sideways scroller (2, 3, 4).
  • Free text and "request changes" hand off to the chat; confirming a prepared action stays in the card (5).
  • Transparent page, host variables with fallbacks, no gradients, mint only on accents and the primary button (6).
  • Three sizes (16, 14, 12 px) and two weights (regular, semibold) (9).
  • Light and dark, AA contrast, keyboard focus rings (10).
  • Works from 320 px, no fixed widths, 44 px targets, nothing flush to an edge (11).
  • Skeletons that match the final layout, not spinners (12).
  • No logo or app name inside the card; the host labels it (13).
  • The pay URL is shown so the person recognises where the button goes (32).
  • Superseded cards grey out, disable their buttons and stop telling the model anything (35).

Fields marked "proposed" are not in the spike's structuredContent yet: the per-freelancer quote list (view: "quotes") and the receipt (view: "receipt", with paidAt). Everything else mirrors the spike's offerings and project views.
