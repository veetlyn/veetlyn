Accessed 2026-10-07. Prices in USD unless noted. Part A covers freelancer tools, marketplaces, paying contractors and gaming commerce. Part B covers digital-product platforms, PayPal's fees and Payleor's running costs. Both parts come from the vendors' own pages unless a row is marked secondary.

Notes:
- Marketplaces earn from a percentage of each order, often on both sides. Fiverr takes about 20% from the seller plus 5.5% and $3.50 from the buyer. Upwork takes 0 to 15% from the freelancer plus 5 to 10% and a contract fee from the client. Malt takes 2 to 10% from the freelancer plus 5 to 20% from the client.
- Seller subscriptions sit on top of the commission and do not replace it: Fiverr Seller Plus costs $15 to $49 a month, and Upwork Freelancer Plus costs $19.99. The subscription buys visibility, bids (Connects) or faster payouts.
- Contra is the exception: 0% commission and a flat fee per payment ($15 or $29) that a $29/mo or $199/yr subscription removes. This is the closest model to "subscription removes the per-payment fee."
- Small-order surcharges exist: Fiverr adds $3.50 under $200, and Upwork adds an initiation fee of up to $4.99 or $14.99.

### 4. Estimated cost per active seller

This is an estimate, not a quote. Assumptions:
- One active seller per month: 50 orders, 5 AI quote drafts (15,000 input and 2,000 output tokens each), 2 GB stored files, 200 downloads, 300 emails, 5,000 page views.
- About 3 Worker requests per page view (HTML plus API calls; static assets are free), 10 requests per order (checkout, capture, webhooks, status), and 2 per download. Total about 16,000 Worker requests at 10 ms CPU each.
- Downloads are one R2 GET each (Class B). About 20 uploads (Class A).
- About 500 queue messages (emails plus webhooks), 3 operations each.
- AI drafts run in a Durable Object for about 60 s each at 128 MB, with about 200 DO requests.
- Payleor pays the model provider (bring-your-own-key means Payleor's own key, not the seller's).
- Hyperdrive is included. Database and email plans are shared across sellers and shown separately.
- Costs are marginal list prices, ignoring included allowances. In practice the first sellers fit inside the included tiers and cost close to $0 in variable usage.

### 5. Gaps

- PayPal: no official statement found on whether PayPal charges the partner for the platform fee amount, on a maximum platform fee, or on Subscriptions API fees beyond standard rates.
- PayPal micropayments: the fees page says sellers "may apply". The application process and any price ceiling are not stated there. The User Agreement section on micropayments was not read.
- PayPal EU or other regional fee pages were not fetched. Only EUR fixed fees shown on the US page are reported.
- PayPal Payouts to Venmo is not listed as a separate Payouts line. Only Hyperwallet shows "Venmo Transfer $0.25".
- Stan Store: transaction fee and processing are not stated on the official help page. The 0% figure comes from secondary sources.
- Payhip and Ko-fi Gold yearly prices: not found.
- Shopify Starter plan: not found on the current pricing page.
- Merchant-of-record status not stated for Payhip, Stan Store and Podia.
- Tax: most pages do not say whether prices exclude tax. Etsy states its fees exclude VAT.
- OpenAI: no current "mini" text model on the pricing page. gpt-6-luna is used as the cheap OpenAI model instead.
- The per-seller estimate uses assumed request counts, CPU time and DO duration. It should be checked against real Workers analytics once traffic exists.
