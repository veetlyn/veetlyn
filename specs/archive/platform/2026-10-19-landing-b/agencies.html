Questions from agencies

How do members get paid?

Each member connects their own PayPal. Their share goes out after your approval, by your rules, or on payday.

We work in Slack or Microsoft Teams. Can we use them?

Not yet. Slack comes after launch. Today, requests come from AI, Discord and your page, and notices go by email.

Can our own agent work with Payleor?

Yes, through the public API and webhooks on Team and Agency. The payer still approves every payment in PayPal.

We are more than 50 people.

Enterprise covers larger teams, several agencies and custom fees. Talk to sales.
