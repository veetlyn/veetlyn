Questions about the network

Is Payleor Network in the Claude or ChatGPT directory?

Not yet. Clients add it by its address. Directory listings come after the platforms' review.

Can the AI pay for my client?

No. The client pays on Payleor and approves the payment in PayPal.

Do I have to be listed?

No. Every offering is listed by default, and you can take any of them off the network.

What does it cost me?

5% once, on the first project with a client the network brings you. Nothing after that.
