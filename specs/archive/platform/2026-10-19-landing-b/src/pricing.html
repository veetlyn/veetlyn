Pricing questions

Do my clients pay anything to Payleor?

No. Clients pay the price you set, through PayPal, without an account. Your fees come out of what you receive.

Is there a fee on payouts to my team?

Payleor adds nothing. PayPal charges its own payout fee, shown before you send.

How do you know a client came from the network?

Their first request starts inside an AI agent through Payleor. The project shows it before you quote.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes, on every plan. Export your projects, clients, payments and files at any time.

Is SEO included?

Web SEO is included in every plan. AI visibility comes with Network membership, which opens by waitlist with manual approval.

Is there a free trial?

14 days on any plan, with no setup fee. You choose a plan before your first client payment.
