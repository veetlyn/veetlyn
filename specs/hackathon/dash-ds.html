Rules

One raised surfaceThe main content is the only raised surface. The sidebar sits on the canvas. The context column is a flat panel inside the surface, in the frame tone, with no elevation.
Frames hold rowsA frame groups rows split by hairlines. Its head or foot is a band. Nothing boxed inside a frame.
Double ruleOnly where hierarchy changes: under the top bar and under a table head.
One primary per viewInk fill for the action that finishes the job. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
Stats as a stripFigures sit in the page header, label above value, no cards.
Words, not dotsStatus is a word. No status dots, no middle-dot separators, no pills.
Warm is troubleAmber and red mean warn and fail, nothing else.
